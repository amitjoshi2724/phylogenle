    ❓ How to Play Phylogenle

    Goal: Deduce the secret organism in as few guesses as possible using evolutionary lineage.

    Each time you guess an organism, Phylogenle calculates two key evolutionary clues:

    1. Diverged (Evolutionary Distance / TMRCA):

    Shows how many Millions of Years Ago (MYA) your guessed organism and the mystery organism diverged from a common ancestor (technically called TMRCA — Time to Most Recent Common Ancestor). The smaller the number, the closer the evolutionary relation!

    2. Lowest Common Ancestor Rank (LCA):

    The most specific taxonomic branch shared between your guess and the secret organism:

    🟩 Species (Exact) 🟨 Genus 🟧 Family 🟥 Order 🟪 Class 🟦 Phylum 🟫 Kingdom

    3. Taxonomic Rank Colors & Solid Square Guide:

    Every guess is color-coded by the lowest common ancestor shared with the target organism:

    🟩
    Species (Match) (0 MYA)
    Green Square — You correctly identified the mystery organism!
    🟨
    Genus Match
    Yellow Square — Direct sister species (e.g. Lion vs Tiger, Dog vs Wolf).
    🟧
    Family Match
    Orange Square — Same Family (e.g. Cat vs Cheetah, Wolf vs Fox).
    🟥
    Order Match
    Red Square — Same Order (e.g. Dog vs Bear, Human vs Chimpanzee vs Lemur).
    🟪
    Class Match
    Purple Square — Same Class (e.g. Mammals, Birds, Reptiles, Insects).
    🟦
    Phylum Match
    Blue Square — Same Phylum (e.g. Chordates, Arthropods, Mollusks).
    🟫
    Kingdom Match
    Brown Square — Same Kingdom (e.g. Animalia, Plantae, Fungi).
    ⬛
    Domain Match
    Black Square — Same Domain (e.g. Eukaryota, Bacteria) or shared cellular life.
    💡
    Clue / Hint Used
    Light Bulb — Recorded chronologically in your guess timeline whenever a hint was revealed.
    ⬜
    Surrender / Gave Up
    White Square — Appended to share row when organism was revealed instead of solved.

    4. Dynamic Phylogenetic Tree:

    Each guess branches dynamically onto the tree on the right. Hover or click on any organism or clade node to read its Wikipedia summary.

    A new daily organism appears every midnight! You can also practice endlessly in Practice mode or replay past puzzles in the Archive.
